Northern Ontario Dev Exchange (NODE) is an Ontario not-for-profit corporation (OCN 1001716490) supporting careers in software development across Northern Ontario. Founded in 2026, NODE is building a network that connects workers, employers, and government partners.

## What NODE runs

NODE supports careers through professional growth events, business attraction initiatives, and a regional network. Our programs include:

- **4 hackathons a year.** Dates and themes are still to be announced.
- **A monthly speaker series.** Twelve sessions at the Norcat Innovation Hub, from September 2026 to August 2027, 6:00 PM to 7:00 PM.
- **Community meetups.** Regular meetups with Cursor Sudbury and GDG Sudbury.
